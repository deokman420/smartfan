Leave on the shelf

  • The DHT22. It stays on the V1 breadboard and is not wired into V2. The SHT40 above replaces it, and the swap is worth shipping on V1 first so that V2 boots an image that has already been proven. Soldering a DHT22 into a ceiling unit means doing the sensor wiring twice, the second time up a ladder.
  • The 24 V industrialPPC, and the IP52 2000 rpm fan that is not the sealed one. The part above is specifically the 12 V IP67 PWM.
  • The 3000 rpm NF-F12. Same screw pattern, louder, and only the step up if a measured duct comes up short. Amazon lists it near $29.95 as well: search.
  • A lint filter. This fan has 0.15 in of pressure, and the easy duct test is already 0.1 in.
  • The SGP41 and an LED ring. Both are later, and the ring does not run from this 1 A buck.
