Where each wire lands

Same kind of drawing as the V1 breadboard sheet: a hole, a color, and the other end. The MOSFET, the buck, the diode, and the resistors are copper on the board already. This page is only the wires you make. Meter readings and the build order stay on the build sheet.

Yellow is not one thing. On the fan, yellow is switched 12 V. On the sensor cable, yellow is SCL, a 3.3 V logic wire, and it splices to the yellow conductor in the short QT pigtail. They leave through different fittings. Landing the fan’s yellow on GP18 destroys the pin.

Click a wire. Pin 1 on every connector is the square pad. A crossing without a dot is not a joint.
